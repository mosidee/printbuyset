Privacy Policy

Print Buy Set (ปริ้นใบเสร็จ) · Effective 9 November 2026
EnglishภาษาไทยSupport

Print Buy Set does not collect any personal data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your images and receipts

Photos, screenshots, PDFs and receipts you print are processed only on your iPhone and are sent only to the Bluetooth printer you choose. They are never uploaded anywhere. If the share sheet hands an image over to the app, it is stored temporarily on your iPhone and deleted once the app opens it.

Bluetooth

The app asks for Bluetooth permission only to find and connect to your receipt printer. It does not use Bluetooth to locate you or to talk to any other device.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your settings (paper width, print adjustments, the printer you last used) and the number of free prints used are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes the settings.

Purchases

The optional unlimited-printing purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: bhumsonj@gmail.com.
